# Domain Verification Standards

## Medical

High-stakes. Prefer current systematic evidence, clinical guidance, regulators/public-health data and peer-reviewed research. Distinguish population evidence from individualized medical advice.

## Legal

High-stakes. Prefer controlling primary legal authority. Record jurisdiction and effective date. Distinguish law, allegation, argument, and commentary.

## Atmospheric

High-stakes when safety is involved. Prefer current warnings and direct observations. Distinguish observation, nowcast, forecast and model scenario.

## Historical

Prefer primary records plus reputable scholarship and archival context. Record translation/version issues when relevant.

## Education

Use documented standards, course/curriculum records and original policy where applicable. Do not infer ability from age or demographic characteristics.

## News/current events

Require freshness, original reporting/records when possible, independence-family analysis, and geographic diversity for cross-border or contested claims.
