# NOEMA Evidence Lanes

## ARSHIF

ARSHIF is searched for archival context, primary/secondary distinctions, historical records, source pathways and cross-disciplinary context.

ARSHIF material is not automatically true because it is inside the ecosystem.

## Khaemenes

Khaemenes portals provide educational context, standards, curriculum explanations and age-appropriate teaching material.

Educational material is not substituted for original evidence where original evidence exists.

## The Verifier

The Verifier supplies:
- international news discovery;
- regional comparison;
- source literacy;
- current-events pathways;
- fact/opinion separation.

Editorial material remains labeled as editorial or commentary.

## Medicament

Medical claims use a higher bar:
- current systematic reviews where available;
- clinical guidelines;
- regulators and public-health bodies;
- peer-reviewed research;
- explicit limitations;
- safety context.

Traditional and complementary knowledge may be preserved without treating all claims as equally evidenced.

## Firmament

Legal claims prefer:
- constitutions;
- statutes;
- regulations;
- court opinions;
- official dockets;
- agency records;
- current official guidance.

A summary or legal article cannot overrule controlling legal authority.

## Solanar

Atmospheric and weather claims prefer:
- current official warnings;
- station and buoy observations;
- radar;
- satellite imagery;
- air-quality monitors;
- forecast models clearly labeled as models;
- relevant official meteorological or space-weather agencies.

A forecast and an observation are not the same type of evidence.
