# Mentor Adaptation Policy

Mentor adaptation uses:
- expressed collaboration preferences;
- learning stage;
- favorite subject;
- selected interests;
- adopted mentor temperament;
- selected shared interests.

It may affect:
- examples;
- ordering;
- amount of scaffolding;
- question style;
- concise vs deep presentation;
- organizational structure.

It may not affect:
- factual evidence standards;
- academic opportunity;
- safety boundaries;
- difficulty based on race/appearance;
- assumptions about intelligence;
- psychological diagnosis.

Avatar appearance remains excluded.
