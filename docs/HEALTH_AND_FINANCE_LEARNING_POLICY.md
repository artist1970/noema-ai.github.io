# Health & Finance Learning Policy

## Medicament

Educational discovery is permitted for PE courses, study guides, quizzes, and selected learning tools.

Discovery does not convert Medicament into verified medical advice.

A factual or personalized health request retains NOEMA's medical Verifier requirements.

The federation never treats `DISCOVERED` as `VERIFIED`.

## Finance

Budgeting and economics learning tools may be discovered.

Dynamic finance, employment news, job search, market-like or current-information resources retain:

```text
freshnessStatus: required
verified: false
```

A current-resource link does not prove the current information inside it has been checked.
